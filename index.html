How to play

  1. Choose any two numbers you still own.
  2. Both players lock in secretly.
  3. Higher total earns one win. A tie earns no wins.
  4. Both discard their two played numbers.
  5. Each takes the larger number from the opponent's submission.
  6. First to 7 wins takes the match. If nobody reaches 7 before a player cannot play, the match is a draw.
